# Skill: Alarm Knowledge Curation

## Responsibility

Suggest organization improvements across validated knowledge fragments before
the deterministic merger writes the final knowledge base.

## Inputs

- Validated fragments
- Duplicate pattern information
- Conflict information

## Output

A curator plan. The plan may propose order, section grouping, and conflict
notes, but it does not write the final knowledge base.

## Guardrails

- Do not change support, purity, entropy, or severity distributions.
- Do not change default severity.
- Do not silently resolve conflicts.
- Leave final writing to the deterministic merger.
